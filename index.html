05 / Questions

Fair questions.

Do I need to be technical?

No — that's the whole point. You get a plain-language video walkthrough and a one-page handoff doc. If you can use email, you can run this.

Why fixed price instead of hourly?

Because hourly billing rewards slow work. A fixed price means I'm incentivized to finish fast and build it right — and you know exactly what it costs before I start.

What tools do you build on?

Mostly n8n, plus Make or Zapier if you're already paying for one — I never force a migration. Your workflows connect to the tools you already use: your email, your CRM, your forms.

What does it cost to run each month?

Usually close to nothing. Most workflows run on tools with generous free tiers. If a workflow uses paid APIs (like SMS or AI calls), you pay actual usage as a passthrough — agreed up front, with a monthly ceiling.

What if it breaks?

Every build includes 30 days of support. On the Autopilot Retainer, everything is monitored and I fix breaks within 48 hours — usually before you notice.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. On final payment, the workflows, credentials, and documentation are 100% yours.
