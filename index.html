05 / Questions

Fair questions.

Who is this for?

Small agencies serving home-service businesses, and service businesses with one specific gap between an inbound enquiry and its follow-up. We check fit and compatibility before agreeing to work.

What do you need from me?

One lead source and destination, an approved mailbox, someone who handles replies, approved wording, and an account owner who can authorize the necessary access. We'll agree on test data and acceptance criteria first.

What does it cost to run?

Third-party software and usage costs depend on your existing accounts and are not included in the installation fee. Any additional cost requires your approval.

What happens if something breaks?

The installation includes 30 calendar days of correction for reproducible defects in the agreed build, with responses during agreed weekday business hours. New features, third-party changes and ongoing monitoring are separately scoped.

Who owns the workflow?

The installation is designed for your client-owned accounts or another arrangement agreed in advance. You'll receive the workflow documentation and handoff; access and ownership are confirmed before the project begins.

Will anything be sent without approval?

No live sending is activated until the workflow, recipients, messages and tests have been reviewed and approved by the client.
